Cheap Green Home Loans

UK households can now get interest-free or low-interest loans to install solar panels, heat pumps and batteries with no upfront cost.

  • Warm Home Loans scheme launched Tuesday across the UK
  • Loans range from £7,500 to £35,000
  • Heat pump grants of up to £9,000 a year may also be available
  • Saving up to £550 a year on bills, plus £300 more with a time-of-use tariff

Why it matters: Lower upfront costs can help more families switch to clean power and make homes cheaper to run.

China Clean Tech Cuts CO2

China’s record exports of clean energy technology are helping reduce global emissions, according to a new report.

  • Exports include solar panels, wind turbines, batteries and electric vehicles
  • The report estimates 450 million metric tons of carbon dioxide will be averted this year
  • The report was released Thursday in Bangkok
  • UN climate talks are set to begin next month in Turkey

Why it matters: Big clean-tech exports can speed the shift away from fossil fuels and cut planet-warming pollution.

ISS Gets Power Boost

Two astronauts completed a spacewalk to install a new solar array on the International Space Station, moving it closer to a major power upgrade.

  • The spacewalk lasted 7 hours and 11 minutes
  • A new solar array was installed on the ISS
  • The final array is due on November 5
  • The station could get up to 30% more available power

Why it matters: More solar power gives the station greater energy capacity and supports its future safe deorbit.
