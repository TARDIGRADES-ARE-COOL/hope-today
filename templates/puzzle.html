      How to play

      One puzzle a day. The answer is the topic of today's good news.

      The clue is a sentence with the answer tucked inside it. Type your guess and press Check; wrong guesses are free.

      Solve it, or give up, and today's good news is yours to read.

      Five tricks

      Each day uses one. Spotting which is half the fun. The number in brackets is the answer's length.

      • Hidden: across words. CAT is in “public attention”.
      • Mix it up: a word, shuffled. HEART becomes EARTH.
      • First letters: Happy Otters Play Everywhere: HOPE.
      • Backwards: “the village” read right to left: LIVE.
      • Two halves: a clue for each half. SUN + FLOWER.

      Hints and streaks

      Hint 1 is free: it names today's trick. Hint 2 describes the word. Hint 3 shows where to look.

      Give up any time and we'll show you how the clue worked.

      Play every day to grow your 🔥 streak, and share your result without spoiling it.
